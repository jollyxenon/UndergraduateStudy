孙可婕
延安大学，中国·陕西 咸阳 716000
摘 要：延安红色文化是中国共产党人精神谱系的重要组成部分，蕴含着丰富的革命历史内涵与育人价值。在数字
技术深度融入社会发展的新时代，借助数字化手段激活延安红色文化资源的当代价值，既是传承红色基因的必然要求，
也是创新思想政治教育的重要路径。论文从延安红色文化的内涵与价值出发，分析当前数字化传播的实践成效与现
实挑战，进而从资源整合、技术赋能、场景融合、生态构建四个维度探索其数字化传播与育人的创新路径，旨在为
新时代红色文化的传承发展提供理论参考与实践借鉴。

Exploration of the Digital Communication and Education Paths of Yan’an Red Cultural Resources
in the New Era
Kejie Sun
Yan’an University, Xianyang, Shaanxi, 716000, China
Abstract: Yan’an red culture is an important part of the spiritual lineage of the Communist Party of China, containing rich
revolutionary historical connotations and educational values. In the new era where digital technology is deeply integrated into
social development, leveraging digital means to activate the contemporary value of Yan’an red cultural resources is not only an
inevitable requirement for inheriting the red gene but also an important path for innovating ideological and political education.
Starting from the connotation and value of Yan’an red culture, this paper analyzes the practical achievements and realistic
challenges of current digital communication, and then explores innovative paths for its digital communication and education from
four dimensions: resource integration, technological empowerment, scene integration, and ecological construction. The aim is to
provide theoretical references and practical experiences for the inheritance and development of red culture in the new era.
Keywords: Yan’an red culture; digital communication; education paths; red gene; digital technology
