
\titledquestion{TA's Grade Grid}[17]

After grading a CS101 assignment, the TAs store the scores in an
$n\times n$ grid
\[
A=(a_{ij}),
\]
where $n$ is a positive integer.

Each row has a unique minimum element. For row $i$, let
\[
m_i=\arg\min_{1\le j\le n} a_{ij}
\]
be the column containing its minimum.

The grading system guarantees that the positions of the row minima are
nondecreasing:
\[
m_1\le m_2\le\cdots\le m_n.
\]

For example, consider the following $4\times4$ grid:
\[
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
12 & 5 & 8 & 10\\
9 & 2 & 7 & 6\\
8 & 7 & 3 & 9\\
11 & 8 & 6 & 1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The minimum elements of the four rows are $5$, $2$, $3$, and $1$,
respectively. Their column positions are
\[
\langle m_1,m_2,m_3,m_4\rangle
=
\langle2,2,3,4\rangle,
\]
which satisfy the nondecreasing condition.

Your goal is to find
\[
m_1,m_2,\ldots,m_n.
\]

Design a divide-and-conquer algorithm that runs in $o(n^2)$ time.



\begin{solution}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(0)] Initialize: Set $l=1$, $r=n$.
    \item[(1)] Solve: Set $\text{mid}=\lfloor\frac{l+r}{2}\rfloor$. Find the minimum element in row $\text{mid}$ by scanning every element. Let $m_{\text{mid}}$ be the column index of this minimum element.
    \item[(2)] Divide: Recursively find the row minima in the upper half $[l,\text{mid}]$ and the lower half $[\text{mid},r]$.
\end{enumerate}

Given the monotonicity of the row minima, we can restrict the search range for the upper half to $[l,\text{mid}]$ and the lower half to $[\text{mid},r]$, so no minima are missed.

$$
T(n) = 2T(n/2) + O(n)
$$

$$
T(n) = O(n \log n) = o(n^2)
$$

\end{solution}
