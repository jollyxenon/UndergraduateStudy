\titledquestion{Karatsuba's Algorithm}
Let $A,B$ are two polynomials where $A(x) = \displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n}a_i x^i, B(x) = \displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n} b_ix^i$.

The goal of this problem is to invent an algorithm to multiply two polynomials faster.

\begin{parts}
\part[1] The time complexity of calculating the product of two polynomials of $n$ or less degree trivially (i.e. use $C(x) = \displaystyle \sum_{0\leq i,j\leq n} a_ib_j x^{i+j}$ ) is \fillin[$O(n^2)$]. \\

\part[3]
Recall the vertical multiplication on polynomials. Rewrite $A(x) = A_1(x)x^{\frac{n}2} + A_2(x)$ and $B(x) = B_1(x)x^{\frac{n}2} + B_2(x)$, where $A_i,B_i$ are polynomials of $\frac n2$ or less degree :\\
\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{rrr}
     &        $A_1x^{\frac{n}2}$& $+A_2$\\
     & $\times B_1x^{\frac{n}2}$& $+B_2$\\ \hline
                & $A_1B_2x^{\frac{n}2}$ & $+A_2B_2$ \\
    $A_1B_1x^n$ & $+A_2B_1x^{\frac{n}2}$ & \\ \hline
    $A_1B_1x^n$ & $+(A_1B_2+A_2B_1)x^{\frac{n}2}$ & $+A_2B_2$.
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
Write down the recurrence relation of this algorithm's time complexity and calculate it:
\begin{solution}

$$
T(n) = 4T\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + \Theta(n)
$$

$$
T(n) = \Theta(n^2)
$$
\end{solution}

\part[3]
    Recall how Strassen's algorithm works for matrix multiplication: It decreases one time of multiplication (from $8$ to $7$), then its time complexity turns into $\Theta($\fillin[$n^{\log_2 7}$]$)$ from $\Theta(n^3)$. \\
    Consider how to reduce one multiplication, we wonder if $A_1B_2+A_2B_1$ can be calculated with $1$ multiplication with proper polynomial calculation: \\
    \textbf{Hint: You can use any number of additions. Think out reuse $A_1B_1$ and $A_2B_2$. ($A_1B_1$ and $A_2B_2$ won't be considered as extra multiplication.)}\\
    $A_1B_2+A_2B_1 = $ \fillin[$(A_1 + A_2)(B_1 + B_2) - A_1 B_1 - A_2 B_2$][7cm]
\part[3]
After the modification, we obtain a method to use $1$ less multiplication to calculate the polynomial multiplication.

Write down the recurrence relation of the modified algorithm's time complexity and calculate it.

\begin{solution}

$$
T(n) = 3T\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + \Theta(n)
$$

$$
T(n) = \Theta(n^{\log_2 3})
$$
\end{solution}

\end{parts}
